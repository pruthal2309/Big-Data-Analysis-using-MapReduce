billie eilish s lone south by southwest performance this year was a back to the future moment for the 17 year old singer both eilish and her brother and collaborator finneas o connell who also opened the saturday night session at the uber eats club made note of the fact that they were in austin two years ago in 2017 they played on the patio of the same venue the former la zona rosa and also caught vince staples on the main stage inside and of course she played burn the track that staples features on from eilish s 2017 ep don t smile at me with his voice on pre recorded track now it s eilish s turn as a headliner however and the anticipation for the march 29 release of her first full album when we all fall asleep where do we go was palpable throughout the club packed so tightly that it was hard to get a good look at the diminutive eilish from anywhere behind the first handful of rows it was also a crowd perfectly in tune with eilish s band of dreamscape pop 50 minutes of an ethereal and unrelentingly languid flow enveloped the o connells delicate melodies only occasionally interrupted by some thick room shaking bass throbs and the more spirited choruses of bury a friend and copycat
