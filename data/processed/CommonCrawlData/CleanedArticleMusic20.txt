on thursday night march 21 leading urban artists gathered at the coliseo de puerto rico to celebrate the first annual tu m sica urban awards ozuna led all artists with six awards followed by daddy yankee and natti natasha with three awards apiece tu m sica celebrated urban music from the place where the genre was born puerto rico the event produced by soraya s nchez and mr mrs entertainment aired live on telemundo puerto rico it will then be broadcasted in the united states on friday march 22 at 9 00 p m est here is the full list of winners artista urbano masculinoozuna
