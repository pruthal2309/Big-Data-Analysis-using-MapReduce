carrie underwood was crowned the winner of the fan voted milestone award presented by chevrolet tonight may 18 at the 2014 billboard music awards i truly have the best fans in the world underwood said emotionally adding that she wouldn t get to do even a zillionth of what she s been able to accomplish if not for all those fans the bbmas second annual milestone award which celebrated the innovation of this year s hitmakers was presented by kelly rowland and a lucky fan who won the opportunity to co present carrie underwood beat out two other finalists onerepublic and ellie goulding on the strength of her many fans votes over the course of the last few weeks the competition began with six nominees imagine dragons john legend luke bryan underwood onerepublic and ellie goulding fan votes narrowed the field to three finalists underwood ellie goulding and onerepublic three weeks ago and tonight live during the bbmas show underwood s fans gave her the win last year s winner was pop prince justin bieber miranda lambert carrie underwood duet on billboard music awards underwood and fellow country star miranda lambert turned in a stellar performance making the television debut of their new duet somethin bad which will appear on lambert s upcoming album platinum due june 3 i love country music underwood said as part of her milestone acceptance speech also thanking god for allowing her to get onstage to perform billboard music awards video the online extension of billboard magazine billboard biz is the essential online destination for the music business learn more 2019 billboard all rights reserved
