by talia lavinjanuary 23 2017in my new secular life i have acted like a roman and stolen my chicken soup from the greeks all chicken soups are alike at the start a dead bird in water boiling flesh a billow of steam grains of salt in the cook s palm growing up i ate ashkenazi jewish chicken soup with sliced celery and parsnips flavored with dill in a pouch of cheesecloth its weekly appearance at our table heralded the sabbath strictly observed in my orthodox family which meant a twenty five hour pause in labor during that time the work in my family was more abstract loving playing making war on one another by saturday afternoon an atlas of purple wine stains decorated the tablecloth i left orthodox judaism when i left home and i ve never made a jewish chicken soup since when i return to my family s table it s as a prodigal wisecracking chain smoking daughter in my new secular life i have acted like a roman and stolen my chicken soup from the greeks it is called avgolemono a broth of chicken thickened with whisked egg and lemon and fortified with rice and spinach the name greek for egg lemon can in mediterranean cuisine refer to a sauce a broth or a thickening agent some sources say it harks back to the time of alexander the great other stories say it came to greece along with sephardic jews expelled from spain in the fifteenth century who even in salonika called it by its ladino name sopa de huevo y lim n i first found a recipe for avgolemono soup when searching for a birthday gift for my mother i was going to cook up a big meal and serve it by candlelight stealing a little of the language of romance i found instructions on the internet and i came up with brandy apples too deglazed in a scary spout of flame but foremost was the soup which i had never tasted before it was pale at the darkened table silky warm in those days i wanted to offer something so perfect that it would placate all parental disappointment something that could garner only praise in the bible the primary text of my religious childhood the one figure who is given soup as a gift has far more taken from him in return in genesis esau is hungry he has hunted all day and he comes to his brother jacob s tent for food jacob gives him lentil stew sometimes translated as pottage mess broth and in exchange the clumsy ruddy esau gives up his firstborn rights he did eat and drink and got up and went on his way the bible says so esau despised his birthright sometimes a gift is treachery sometimes you have to give up your birthright and turn away from the tastes you knew at twenty seven i have not yet learned to cook for just myself for seven years when i cooked at all i cooked for the man who became my husband when i was twenty four at our wedding the food was good i don t remember what we ate but i remember that we continued eating throughout the toasts at home in the years that followed he was impatient with my need to roast a whole chicken he could do up the thighs so well with oxtail spice or paprika salmon was his favorite with butter and dill i made it for birthdays and anniversaries we were the kind of people who arrived at a party with a bowl of chopped liver and a pitcher of hurricane cocktails in sticky pink we were married for two years enough time to acquire a kitchenaid mixer but not enough to acquire children enough time to share a studio apartment but not enough to buy a house when i moved out i took the pitcher shaped like an elephant and most of the platters but i left in such a hurry that half my books remained since my divorce i have begun to issue too many dinner invitations although my kitchen shared with three housemates is cramped and for months until a friend intervened with screws my grandmother s walnut table wobbled perilously at the slightest touch nonetheless i select recipes and falsely casual induce my friends to join me come over we ll make lemon drop cookies i say sift confectioner s sugar into a bowl whisk in lemon juice add still more sugar to thicken the blandishments of my loneliness are sweet and acidic brushed on carefully with a fork stay for dinner i ll make chicken in solitude my plate blue le creuset ceramic a wedding gift is as empty as my bed some months after the divorce i invited more friends than could fit into my tiny living room for a dinner i laid out a card table that jutted into the kitchen arranging folding chairs whose backs knocked the walls and caught at the door s handle i made scalloped potatoes i blanched asparagus i whacked at slivered almonds with a hardcover book in an attempt to make a cake that was later described by my guests not unkindly as a granola or a brittle the friends i invited had seen me sob in broad daylight when my husband first left they had fielded calls at unreasonable hours in return i made them avgolemono when the chicken has simmered for an hour add a cup of long grain rice orzo is more traditionally greek but any rice will do then comes the trickiest step tempering the eggs it s a magician s flick of the wrist the small skill that makes the soup a gift and a secret juice two lemons and beat three eggs in a bowl take a ladleful of hot broth and whisk it into the yellow mixture repeat this action beating in ladle after ladle until your fingertip tells you that the liquid is hot then turn off the heat on your pot of broth and pour in the lemon and egg chase it to the far reaches with the whisk until the broth clouds as suddenly as a summer storm don t let the mixture boil or the whites will turn to albuminous ribbons the yolks to rubble and the lovely color like a newly painted parlor will be wrecked be careful and be gentle shred the chicken s flesh salt it let it slide back into the broth at the last possible moment drop a handful of spinach to wilt in the steam let the pot feed as many mouths as possible dip the ladle until it scrapes the bottom clean when the final guest has left and you re doing the dishes take up the last spoonful a small gift to yourself let the fine sour taste linger on your tongue dive into the world of literature with the new yorker s weekly fiction newsletter by ben taubby mike spiesby alexander the great other stories say it came to greece along with sephardic jews expelled from spain in the fifteenth century who even in salonika called it by its ladino name sopa de huevo y lim n i first found a recipe for avgolemono soup when searching for a birthday gift for my mother i was going to cook up a big meal and serve it by candlelight stealing a little of the language of romance i found instructions on the internet and i came up with brandy apples too deglazed in a scary spout of flame but foremost was the soup which i had never tasted before it was pale at the darkened table silky warm in those days i wanted to offer something so perfect that it would placate all parental disappointment something that could garner only praise in the bible the primary text of my religious childhood the one figure who is given soup as a gift has far more taken from him in return in genesis esau is hungry he has hunted all day and he comes to his brother jacob s tent for food jacob gives him lentil stew sometimes translated as pottage mess broth and in exchange the clumsy ruddy esau gives up his firstborn rights he did eat and drink and got up and went on his way the bible says so esau despised his birthright sometimes a gift is treachery sometimes you have to give up your birthright and turn away from the tastes you knew at twenty seven i have not yet learned to cook for just myself for seven years when i cooked at all i cooked for the man who became my husband when i was twenty four at our wedding the food was good i don t remember what we ate but i remember that we continued eating throughout the toasts at home in the years that followed he was impatient with my need to roast a whole chicken he could do up the thighs so well with oxtail spice or paprika salmon was his favorite with butter and dill i made it for birthdays and anniversaries we were the kind of people who arrived at a party with a bowl of chopped liver and a pitcher of hurricane cocktails in sticky pink we were married for two years enough time to acquire a kitchenaid mixer but not enough to acquire children enough time to share a studio apartment but not enough to buy a house when i moved out i took the pitcher shaped like an elephant and most of the platters but i left in such a hurry that half my books remained since my divorce i have begun to issue too many dinner invitations s a magician s flick of the wrist the small skill that makes the soup a gift and a secret juice two lemons and beat three eggs in a bowl take a ladleful of hot broth and whisk it into the yellow mixture repeat this action beating in ladle after ladle until your fingertip tells you that the liquid is hot then turn off the heat on your pot of broth and pour in the lemon and egg chase it to the newly painted parlor will be wrecked be careful and be gentle shred the chicken s flesh salt it let it slide back into the broth at the last possible moment drop a handful of spinach to wilt in the
